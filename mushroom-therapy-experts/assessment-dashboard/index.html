Psilocybin Readiness Assessment Test

Know where you stand before you seek care.

A 24-item interactive screen for talking with a clinician about regulated psilocybin-assisted therapy. It cannot diagnose, clear you medically, or recommend a dose.

If you are in crisis or not safe right now, contact local emergency services or a crisis line before continuing.

How scoring works

  • STOP flags override everything — pause and seek clinical care.
  • Readiness points sum protective and caution answers (max 32).
  • CONDITIONAL-GO only means it may be reasonable to seek regulated intake — still not clearance.
